How it works

Simple, clear, and designed to save your time.

  1. 1. You send a short request

    Tell me what your business does and what you need (new page, small website, link page, updates, or fixes). If you already have a website, include the link.

  2. 2. I build a first version

    I create a draft based on your request and send it to you to review. You can check it on your phone and suggest any changes.

  3. 3. Review and publish

    We make small edits if needed, then the website goes live and is ready to share with your clients.

Most small projects are completed within a few days.
